      About DEEPGUARD AI

      DEEPGUARD AI is an AI-powered deepfake video detection and digital media forensics platform built on OpenCV face tracking, a ResNeXt-50 spatial encoder, an LSTM temporal model and Binary Focal Loss.

      Limitations

      • Video only. Image and audio detection are not implemented.
      • Confidence is the model's score for its own verdict, not a guaranteed accuracy.
      • Without a trained checkpoint the app runs in demo mode (rule-based heuristic).
      • Quality depends on face visibility, compression, lighting and how closely the clip resembles training data.
      • Treat results as one indicator in a forensic review, not as proof.
